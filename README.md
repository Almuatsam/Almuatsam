### 🚀 About Me

I'm a Computer Science graduate from Muscat, Oman, focused on UI/UX and product design  I like turning ideas into clean, user-centred interfaces. My long-term goal is blending code with fashion and sewing design.

🔭 &nbsp;I'm currently working on **A multi-tenant SaaS business management system for cafés/retail**  
🌱 &nbsp;I'm currently learning **Multi-tenancy architecture &amp; scalable backend design**  
👯 &nbsp;I'm looking to collaborate on **UI/UX and front-end projects, especially anything blending design + AI**  
💬 &nbsp;Ask me about **React, Figma, UI/UX design systems**  
😄 &nbsp;Pronouns: **He/Him**  
⚡ &nbsp;Fun fact: **I'm also an artist . I work with sewing materials and painting, inspired by Cubism and bright colors**
